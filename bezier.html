ECG shapes from rational Bézier segments

Grey dots are control points. The red dashed polygon shows the points of the current segment the dot is tracing.

Segment: — n = — t = 0.00
$$\mathbf{R}(t)=\frac{\sum_{i=0}^{n} w_i\,\mathbf{p}_i\,B_i^n(t)}{\sum_{i=0}^{n} w_i\,B_i^n(t)},\quad B_i^n(t)=\binom{n}{i}t^i(1-t)^{n-i}$$
Tip: QRS looks “spiky” because a few points have large weights \(w_i\). Control-point size is loosely weight-based.
