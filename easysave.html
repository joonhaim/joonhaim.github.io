Personalized Savings Plans & Daily Budget Generator

EasySave analyzes your income and past spending to craft a custom savings roadmap. Rather than one‑size‑fits‑all advice, you get recommendations that adapt to exactly how and when you spend. Every morning, you’ll see a precise ‘spend‑up‑to’ amount for the day, calculated from your goals and historic habits. Think of it as your pocket‑sized spending coach.

Interactive Dashboard

Log expenses on the fly and watch your dashboard update in real time—charts, progress bars, and peer‑benchmark markers all refresh as soon as you hit ‘Save’.

Goal Tracking & Visual Progress

Set weekly or monthly milestones and see exactly how close you are to hitting each target. Our visual trackers turn abstract numbers into tangible steps.

Spending Analysis

Dive deep into your habits with categorized bar and line charts. Filter by date range or category to uncover where and how you might be able to trim.

AI-Powered Predictions

Let our ARIMA‑based engine forecast your next month’s spending so you can adjust today. It’ll even flag categories where you’re off track to meet your goals.

Profile & Settings

Manage your account details, switch themes or languages, and customize notification preferences, all from one tidy settings pane.
