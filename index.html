New Project

EduPace: temporary pacemaker training simulator

A TU Delft biomedical engineering project that blends a web-based ECG simulator with tactile hardware to rehearse pacing configuration, sensing, and capture thresholds.

  • Realistic ECG modeling with scenario-based learning flows.
  • Hardware control unit that mirrors temporary pacemaker workflows.
  • Built for repeatable training and consistent clinical readiness.
